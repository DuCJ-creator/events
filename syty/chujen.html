Chujen Elementary School Bilingual Experience @ Chung Hsin School (115 Academic Year)

課程設計核心理念 (Core Concepts)

本課程由忠信學校 FRP雙語教案為竹仁國小「雙語課程體驗」專案設計，採 40 分鐘精準節奏架構。結合動手實作（Hands-on DIY）、自然科學實驗、樂高程式控制與音樂感知，引導學生在沉浸式雙語情境中建立跨領域核心素養。
This curriculum is designed by Chung Hsin School FRP Bilingual Lesson Plans for the "Bilingual Course Experience" project tailored for Chujen Elementary School. Designed in a precise 40-minute structure, it integrates hands-on DIY, natural science experiments, Lego robotics coding, and musical perception to build cross-disciplinary core competencies.

領域重點 (Domain)

Miguel 老師（搭配外師 Teacher Miguel & Foreign Teacher）
13項指定食材 (13 Designated Ingredients)

40分鐘教學流程 (40-Min Activity Flow)

    Warm-up: 導入 SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) 與 Power Up 能量概念

  • 7m
    Stage 3: 食育討論（高能量密度點心對急難救援的作用）
    Discuss the role of high-energy snacks in disaster relief.
  • 5m
    Wrap-up: 品嚐能量棒與雙語學習單填寫
    Tasting session and completing bilingual worksheets.

40分鐘教學流程 (40-Min Activity Flow)
