01 / FORMAT

甄選流程

全程約 5–10 分鐘，共分四個部分。Total length: 5–10 minutes, in four parts.

01

自我介紹Introduction

1 MIN

以英文簡短自我介紹。
Brief self-introduction in English.

02

預備演講Prepared Speech

2–3 MIN

就事先公布的題目擇一回應。
Respond to one of the provided topics in advance.

03

即席問答Impromptu Question

2–3 MIN

回答現場提出的題目。
Answer a question given on the spot.

04

追問對談Follow-Up Q&A

1–2 MIN

與評審進行簡短對話。
Short dialogue with the evaluators.

英語溝通能力Clarity, pronunciation, vocabulary
組織架構Logical flow, structure, transitions
批判性思考Analyze and defend a point
台風表現Confidence, pace, voice, eye contact
互動態度Respectful and responsive in Q&A
02 / TOPICS

預備演講題目

選擇所屬組別的題目，清楚表達你的立場與理由。Choose the topic for your division and make your case.

初中組 · Junior High School

Is it better to read a book or watch a movie?

看書比較好，還是看電影比較好？

高中組 · High School

Should governments regulate artificial intelligence as strictly as weapons?

政府是否應該像管制武器一樣，嚴格規範人工智慧？

03 / HOW TO JOIN

參與流程

從主動報名開始，帶著準備好的想法來到甄選現場。Start with initiative. Arrive prepared to speak.

  1. 自願自發，主動報名Join voluntarily, on your own initiative
  2. 與父母商議，取得認可與首肯Discuss with your parents and get their approval
  3. 向班導師報名 9/14（一）前Sign up with your homeroom teacher by Mon, Sep 14
  4. 依甄選流程準備甄選內容Prepare your audition following the format above
  5. 於甄選當日出席參加Attend the audition on your scheduled date

AFTER THE AUDITION

甄選通過後，課程將於 10 月開始，上課時間統一為夜讀時間 6:40–8:40，實際上課時間將依隊員與教練情況再行確定。If selected, classes begin in October during evening study hall (6:40–8:40 PM); the exact schedule will be confirmed with the team and coach.
