Purpose

  • Celebrate ChungHsin Culture Month through students’ own stories.
  • Encourage students to reflect on and share their life at CHHS in English.
  • Build clear, confident and engaging public speaking.

Procedure

  • The speaking order is drawn live on stage.
  • Each contestant delivers a 2-minute speech on “My ChungHsin Life.”
  • Time is shown on screen; a soft chime marks the last 30 seconds.
  • The speech is cut right away when time is up.
  • Judges score every speech against the rubric below.

Speech Contest Judging Rubric

1. Content & Structure

  • Relevance to TopicContents connect clearly to “My ChungHsin Life,” with insights or anecdotes related to his/her life at CHHS.
  • Clarity & CoherenceIdeas are organized logically and with a clear structure.
  • Originality & CreativityContents show unique perspectives or memorable details about his/her life at CHHS.

2. Language Use & Grammar

  • Grammar & VocabularyFree of grammatical errors, appropriate and varied vocabulary; natural flow of ideas.

3. Delivery & Engagement

  • Confidence & PoiseAppears confident, maintains eye contact, and uses appropriate body language.
  • Vocal ExpressionUses tone, pitch, and pace effectively to enhance impact; clear pronunciation, and correct intonation.
  • Audience EngagementConnects with the audience, making the content relatable or impactful.

4. Time Management

  • Adherence to Time LimitFinishes within 2 minutes (point deductions for exceeding or undercutting the time limit by over 15 seconds). The speech will be cut right away when time is up.

5. Overall Impact

  • Memorability & EffectivenessLeaves a strong impression; the main message is memorable.
