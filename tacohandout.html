Where is Ensenada? – Geography & Tourism
恩塞納達在哪裡？——地理與旅遊魅力

Location／位置： Baja California, northwestern Mexico, Pacific coast.
墨西哥西北部，下加利福尼亞州，濱臨太平洋。

Distance／距離： 1.5 hours from US-Mexico border. 距離美墨邊境約1.5小時車程。

Known as the "Fish Taco Capital of the World" ／ 被譽為「世界魚塔可之都」。

Why is Ensenada a Food Tourism Capital?
為什麼恩塞納達是美食旅遊聖地？

  • Fresh seafood: boat to table in under 2 hours
    新鮮海產直送：漁船到餐廳不到2小時
  • Valle de Guadalupe wine region (90% of Mexico's wine)
    結合下加州葡萄酒產區（產出墨西哥90%的葡萄酒）
  • High density of taco stands and street food culture
    街頭美食文化豐富，塔可攤密度極高
  • Annual Ensenada Taco Festival
    每年舉辦「恩塞納達塔可節」

The Origin Story of the Ensenada Fish Taco
恩塞納達魚塔可的誕生故事

  • 1950s: Local vendors first placed fried fish into a corn tortilla
    1950年代：當地攤販首次將炸魚放入玉米餅
  • Influenced by Japanese & Chinese immigrant frying techniques
    受日本與中國移民的炸魚技術影響
  • Later added shredded cabbage, creamy sauce, and salsa
    後來加入高麗菜絲、酸奶油醬、莎莎醬
  • Became the classic combination we know today
    形成今日經典組合
✨ Tourism Highlight – Mercado Negro (Black Market) 旅遊亮點：黑市
Fresh fish auction · Live taco stalls · Observe fishermen, chefs, tourists
漁獲直銷拍賣 · 現炸塔可攤位 · 觀察漁民、主廚、觀光客互動
Tourism application: 帶團體驗「從魚網到餐桌」的過程 — "net-to-table" experience.

Step-by-Step Workshop (Part 1)
實作步驟（前半）—— 醃魚與調製麵糊

Step 1 – Marinate the fish ／ 醃魚 (5-8 minutes)
1. Cut fish into strips (2 inches long). 將魚肉切成長約5公分的條狀。
2. Mix with lime juice, minced garlic, salt, chili powder. 混合萊姆汁、大蒜、鹽、辣椒粉。
3. Let sit for 5 min while preparing batter. 靜置5分鐘（同時準備麵糊）。
Step 2 – Make the batter ／ 調製麵糊 (5 minutes)
1. Whisk beer, flour, baking powder. 攪拌啤酒、麵粉、泡打粉。
2. Consistency: thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. 麵糊濃度能附著在湯匙背面。
⚠️ Food safety: Keep raw fish separate from ready-to-eat toppings. 生魚與即食配料請分開處理。

Step-by-Step Workshop (Part 2)
實作步驟（後半）—— 炸魚與組裝

Step 3 – Fry the fish ／ 炸魚 (under instructor supervision, 10-12 min rotation)
▪ Heat oil to 180°C (350°F). 油加熱至180°C。
▪ Dip fish in batter, let excess drip. 裹麵糊，滴落多餘。
▪ Carefully place into hot oil, fry 2–3 min until golden. 小心放入油鍋，炸2-3分鐘至金黃。
▪ Remove with tongs, drain on paper towels. 用夾子取出，紙巾吸油。
⚠️ Safety: No running near fryers / Use tongs / Instructor controls oil.
Step 4 – Warm the tortillas ／ 加熱玉米餅
On a dry pan or griddle, 30 seconds per side. 乾鍋或煎盤加熱，每面約30秒。
Step 5 – Assemble the taco ／ 組裝塔可 (順序由下至上)
① Warm tortilla 玉米餅 → ② Shredded cabbage 高麗菜絲 → ③ Fried fish 炸魚 → ④ Creamy sauce 酸奶油醬 → ⑤ Salsa 莎莎醬 → ⑥ Squeeze of lime 萊姆汁 → ⑦ Optional toppings 可選配料
