A focused briefing for teachers and contestants — schedules, protocols, and practice resources.
For team teachers帶隊老師｜Key reminders
  • 出發前雙證件 / Bring both documents: 帶齊紙本參賽證（A4 單面列印）與附照片身分證件；單字賽現場不補發。
    Bring the printed entry pass and a photo ID. Replacement passes are not issued on site.
  • 跨區考場 / Two venues: 作文、演講在苗栗竹南高中；單字賽在彰化女中。
    Essay and speech events are at Zhunan Senior High; vocabulary is at Changhua Girls’ Senior High.
  • 演講留考 / Stay after speaking: 即席演講結束後須留考至講評，請備好飲水與點心。
    Contestants must remain until the briefing ends; water and a light snack are recommended.
For contestants參賽學生｜Avoid these pitfalls
  • 單字書寫格式 / Writing format: 使用藍、黑墨水筆與印刷體；除專有名詞外一律小寫。
    Use blue or black ink and print clearly. Lowercase is required except for proper nouns.
  • 禁攜電子設備 / No electronics: 手機、智慧錶等裝置須關機並集中存放。
    Turn off and store phones, smartwatches, and other devices.
  • 不得提前離場 / No early departure: 單字與演講皆禁止提早離席，否則視同放棄資格。
    Leaving early from either event means forfeiting eligibility.

Central District · Changhua Girls’ SHS
Strict check-in protocol進場規定（極嚴格）
10:00 前 / by 10:00 進場並完成查驗。
10:10 開考 / starts at 10:10，逾時禁止入場。
參賽證不補發；開考後不得提早離場。
No late entry, replacement pass, or early departure.
Preparation focus備考方向
  • 測驗含拼寫與聽力拼字，熟悉 7000 單字為基礎。
    Prepare for spelling and dictation; build a 7,000-word foundation.
  • 注意英美語拼法差異與語音辨識度。
    Watch regional spelling differences and sound recognition.
  • 多做模擬聽寫，練習印刷體小寫。
    Practise timed dictation and clear lowercase printing.

英語演講比賽
English Speech Competition
115 年 11 月 10 日（二）下午 · Tuesday afternoon, November 10
北 3 區 · 竹南高中
North District 3 · Zhunan SHS
Check-in protocol進場規定
依准考證報到時段準時至竹南高中報到。
Report at the time shown on your pass.
即席演講每 2 分鐘 / 2 minutes 依序號入場。
演講後須留考至休息區，擅自離席視同棄賽。
Preparation focus備考方向
  • 包含指定題演講與看圖即席演講兩項。
    Prepare for the set speech and impromptu picture talk.
  • 即席演講著重臨場反應、說故事與台風。
    Focus on responsiveness, storytelling, delivery, and poise.
  • 多看時事漫畫、統計圖表，快速拉出 3 個主軸。
    Use cartoons and charts to practise finding three angles quickly.
