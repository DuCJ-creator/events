01

A note before we begin · 開始之前

To make sure you understand what is expected of you—and what you can expect from Tr. Shirley during your senior high school English learning journey,read this bilingual guide carefully and review it regularly. More importantly, make the promises and keep them.

為了讓你清楚了解老師對你的期待，也知道你可以從老師這裡得到什麼， 請仔細讀懂這份雙語指南，保持常看常新。更重要的是：做出承諾，信守承諾！

About

Responsibility

關於責任

S

Student’s promise 學生的承諾

I understand that my parents and teachers will offer guidance, supervision, and support. However, I—and I alone—am ultimately responsible for my future and my life. The choices I make and the way I act will shape what I achieve.

我明白，父母和老師會給予我教導、督促與支持；然而，最終能為我的未來 與人生負責的，只有我自己。我的每一個選擇與行動，都將塑造我最後的成就。

  • 01 follow my teacher’s guidance; 聽從老師的教導；
  • 02 listen attentively in class; 上課專心聽講；
  • 03 complete every assignment on time and to the best of my ability; 按時並盡己所能完成每一項作業；
  • 04 give my best effort to every task; and 全力以赴完成每一項任務；並且
  • 05 keep learning, reflecting, and improving. 持續學習、反思，讓自己變得更好。
T

Teacher’s promise 老師的承諾

I understand that a teacher’s essential role is to share wisdom, impart knowledge, and answer questions. My responsibility is to guide, support, and encourage my students so that they can grow and achieve more.

我明白，教師的核心使命是「傳道、授業、解惑」。我的責任是引導、 支持並鼓勵學生，陪伴他們持續成長，走得更遠。

  • 01 communicate the syllabus, curriculum, and assessment system clearly; 清楚說明教學大綱、課程內容與評量方式；
  • 02 prepare thoroughly for every lesson; 用心準備每一堂課；
  • 03 present knowledge and skills clearly; 清晰傳授知識與技能；
  • 04 design meaningful tasks with an appropriate workload; 設計質量兼具、份量合宜的學習任務；
  • 05 provide timely feedback and use it to refine my teaching; and 及時提供回饋，並據此調整教學；以及
  • 06 help you learn how to learn and become an independent learner. 引導你學會學習，成為獨立自主的學習者。

About

Respect

關於尊重

S

Student’s promise 學生的承諾

I understand that respect means accepting others for who they are, even when they are different from me or when we disagree. In and out of class, I will respect my classmates and teacher by listening patiently to their views and needs while expressing my own openly and thoughtfully.

我明白，尊重就是接納彼此的不同，即使我們看法不一。無論課堂內外， 我都會耐心傾聽同學與老師的觀點和需求，也會坦誠而有分寸地表達自己。

T

Teacher’s promise 老師的承諾

I understand that respect is earned, not demanded. You respect me not simply because I am a teacher, but because I strive to be your teacher: one who understands her responsibilities, respects your unique talents, interests, and personality, and creates harmony without demanding uniformity.

我明白，尊重不是索取得來的，而是用行動贏得的。你尊重我，不只是因為 我是一名教師，更因為我努力成為真正屬於你們的老師：明白自身責任， 尊重每個人的天賦、興趣與個性，在差異中建立和諧。

Habits

關於習慣

S

Student’s promise 學生的承諾

I understand that first I shape my habits, and then my habits shape me. The secret of success lies in what I choose to do each day.

我明白，起初是我塑造習慣，而後習慣將塑造我。成功的秘密， 就藏在每天看似細微的選擇裡。

  • 01 plan my learning proactively; 主動規劃學習；
  • 02 take risks and not be afraid of making mistakes; 勇於嘗試，不害怕犯錯；
  • 03 ask questions and seek feedback promptly; 及時提問並主動尋求回饋；
  • 04 practise beyond the classroom and keep the momentum going; 把練習延伸到課堂之外，保持學習動能；
  • 05 track my learning and progress; 觀察並記錄自己的學習進程；
  • 06 take thoughtful notes and review them regularly; and 認真做筆記並定期複習；以及
  • 07 learn English in active and engaging ways. 以主動、投入且有趣的方式學習英語。
T

Teacher’s promise 老師的承諾

I understand the power of habits: good habits open the door to success, while poor habits quietly lead us away from it. I will model positive learning habits and help you build routines you can sustain.

我深知習慣的力量：好習慣為成功開門，壞習慣則讓人不知不覺偏離目標。 我會以身作則，也會陪你建立真正能長久維持的學習習慣。

  • 01 set SMART learning goals; 設定 SMART 學習目標；
  • 02 turn those goals into clear, practical plans; 把目標化為清楚可行的計畫；
  • 03 make repetition purposeful and enjoyable; 讓重複練習變得有意義、有趣；
  • 04 stay flexible when circumstances change; and 在環境改變時保持彈性；以及
  • 05 build a strong network of support. 建立一個有力的支持系統。

About

Discipline

關於紀律

S

Student’s promise 學生的承諾

I understand that nothing worthwhile can be achieved without sound standards and boundaries. As a member of a community, I sometimes need to regulate my behaviour, put the common good before selfish impulses, and set aside personal preferences.

我明白，「不依規矩，不成方圓」。身為群體的一員，我有時需要節制自己的 行為，把共同利益放在一己之便之前，也懂得適時放下個人偏好。

  • 01 follow school policies and classroom expectations so that everyone can learn fairly in a positive, well-ordered environment; and 遵守校規與課堂約定，讓每個人都能在公平、正向且有秩序的 環境中學習；並且
  • 02 do what I need to do before doing what I feel like doing. 先完成應該做的事，再做自己想做的事。
T

Teacher’s promise 老師的承諾

I understand that I must discipline myself before asking the same of my students. I will establish clear classroom expectations, explain their purpose, and apply them consistently and fairly.

我明白，要求學生自律之前，我必須先律己。我會建立清楚的課堂約定， 說明其目的，並以一致而公平的方式實行。

  • 01 Respect one another and listen when someone is speaking. 彼此尊重；他人發言時，專心傾聽。
  • 02 Come to class and every task well prepared. 為每一堂課與每一項任務做好準備。
  • 03 Be punctual and manage time wisely. 準時守時，妥善管理時間。
  • 04 Share, collaborate, and help one another succeed. 樂於分享、協作共學。
  • 05 Keep a constructive attitude and treat others with kindness. 保持正向態度，友善待人。

Effort

關於努力

S

Student’s promise 學生的承諾

I understand that everything is difficult before it becomes easy, which is why steady effort matters. I also understand that effort can matter more than talent: hard work beats talent when talent does not work hard.

我明白，萬事起頭難，因此持續而穩定的努力格外重要。我也明白， 努力往往比天賦更關鍵：當天賦不肯努力，努力終將超越天賦。

  • 01 Whatever result I achieved in the junior-high English exam belongs to the past. The effort I make from today onward will shape my future. 無論國中教育會考的英語成績如何，那都只代表過去； 從今天起付出的努力，才會塑造我的未來。
  • 02 Learning is like rowing upstream: if I stop moving forward, I drift back. 學如逆水行舟，不進則退。
  • 03 No one can force me to learn. Genuine, effective learning begins when motivation comes from within and I choose to act. 沒有人能逼我真正學會。真實而有效的學習， 始於內在的動機與自發的行動。
  • 04 If I cannot fly, I will run. If I cannot run, I will walk. If I cannot walk, I will crawl—but whatever I do, I will keep moving forward. 如果不能飛，我就跑；如果不能跑，我就走；如果不能走， 我就爬。無論如何，我都會繼續向前。
T

Teacher’s promise 老師的承諾

Whatever your starting point, I will remain positive, supportive, encouraging, and ready to inspire you. Growth takes time; even sunlight takes about 8.3 minutes to reach Earth. Be patient, enjoy learning, and keep moving forward.

無論你的起點在哪裡，我都會給予正向的支持、鼓勵與啟發。 成長需要時間；就連陽光抵達地球，也要走約 8.3 分鐘。 請保持耐心、享受學習，並繼續向前。

  • 01 help you understand the value of effort, motivation, attitude, and perseverance; and 幫助你理解努力、動機、態度與毅力的價值；並且
  • 02 show you how small, consistent improvements create a powerful compound effect—the 1% principle. 帶你看見微小而持續的進步如何產生強大的複利效應—— 0.01 法則。

One final thought · 最後

“Do something today that your
future self will thank you for.”

請讓未來的你，能夠感謝今天拼命努力的你。

ChungHsin may once have felt like your “next-best choice.” From now on, let it become your best choice—not because the school itself has changed, but because you have chosen to make the very best of your time here.

也許忠信曾是你當初「退而求其次」的選擇。從現在起，請讓它成為 你最好的選擇——不是因為學校突然改變，而是因為你決定珍惜在這裡 的每一天，成為最好的自己。

Work with purpose. Rest without regret. Keep becoming.

努力有方向，休息無愧疚，持續成為更好的自己。
